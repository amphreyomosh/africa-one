Our Mission

Nurturing every child's
potential since 1983.

DOT Tot Center is a non-profit, parent-operated organization in the heart of downtown Sacramento — close to public transportation and just steps from the Capitol Building.

Our mission is to provide a safe, welcoming place where children can explore the world, expand their minds, express themselves, and build the confidence to become independent, self-assured people.

Programs

Our Classes

Four developmentally designed programs, each tailored to a specific stage of early childhood growth.

View Tuition & Fees

Pooh's Corner

6 weeks – 12 months
Gentle, loving care for infants, with an emphasis on sensory exploration, secure bonding, and reaching developmental milestones in a warm, safe environment.
Learn more

The Elmos

12 months – 2 years
Active, nurturing care for curious toddlers focused on language development, creative play, and building the first set of social skills.
Learn more

The Tigger Bugs

2 – 3 years
Energetic, exploratory learning for 2-year-olds with a structured curriculum that encourages independence and creative expression.
Learn more

The Honey Bees

3 – 4 years
A kindergarten-readiness program emphasizing early literacy, numeracy, problem-solving, and cooperative group learning.
Learn more
