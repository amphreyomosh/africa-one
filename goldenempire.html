Golden Empire Basset Rescue is a volunteer-run nonprofit finding loving homes for abandoned, abused, and unwanted basset hounds across Kern, Tulare, San Luis Obispo, and Inyo counties.

Our Mission

Compassion turned into community service.

Founded in 2007 by lifelong animal lover Jill Peterson, GEBR has spent nearly two decades finding forever homes for the breed she adored most.

Our hounds come to us from shelters and owner relinquishments. They receive veterinary care that includes necessary vaccinations, heartworm testing, and spay/neuter procedures — often at significant cost to our small nonprofit.

Each basset is typically placed into a volunteer foster home where their temperament, social needs, and any behavioral quirks are gently observed. From there, we work to match each hound with the right family — one where the special bond between companion animal and household can truly blossom.

Today, the Board of Directors continues the work Jill began, honoring her legacy one wagging tail at a time.

About GEBR

Carrying on a founder’s legacy.

Jill Peterson, the founder and president of Golden Empire Basset Rescue, passed away on May 31, 2014. Jill was a lifelong animal lover, and her compassion turned into community service when she launched GEBR — a nonprofit serving the Central California area.

The current Board of Directors has pledged to continue the rescue of these remarkable, long-eared hounds. We’re proud to be a member of West Coast BROAD (Basset Rescue Organizations Allied Division), a network of 501(c)(3) organizations working together to ensure every possible hound is rescued.

The Adoption Journey

From shelter intake to forever home.

Each hound moves through a careful process designed to find the right match — for the dog and the family.

Intake

Hounds come to us from shelters and owner relinquishments across Central California.

Vet Care

Vaccinations, heartworm testing, and spay/neuter — plus any medical care needed.

Foster Home

Volunteers evaluate temperament, social needs, and how each hound does with kids, cats, or other dogs.

Forever Family

We thoughtfully match each hound with the right home — one where the bond can truly grow.
