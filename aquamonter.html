A Davis Swim Community Like No Other

The Davis AquaMonsters are a Davis-based competitive swim team where kids learn to swim, cultivate friendships, compete, and have fun in the water. We offer Summer Recreation and USA Swimming programs built for every skill level.

01
Inclusive Competition

Healthy competition from a place of fun and personal development — for every swimmer at every level.

02
Community First

More than a team — a close-knit family of swimmers, parents, coaches, and volunteers united by the water.

03
Lifelong Skills

From first strokes to collegiate pathways — we build swimmers who are confident in and out of the pool.

Three Levels.
One Monster Team.

Whether your child is just getting comfortable in the water or racing at the national level, we have a program built for them.

Program 01
Ages 4–7 · Summer, Fall & Spring
Little Monsters

For our youngest swimmers. We focus on water comfort, listening skills, kicking strength, and developing freestyle and backstroke. The perfect launchpad for a lifetime of swimming.

Learn More →
Program 02
Ages 5–8 · Year-Round
Big Monsters

Expanding on the Little Monsters foundation with near year-round instruction and exercise. Builds endurance, stroke technique, and the competitive spirit that makes AquaMonsters great.

Learn More →
Program 03
Ages 8–18 · Year-Round
AquaMonsters

Our flagship program. Summer Recreation and USA Swimming tracks for dedicated competitors. Top swimmers train all 4 strokes and compete at regional and national levels.

Learn More →

Your Journey to the Pool

01
Register Online

Complete your online registration and select the right program for your swimmer's age and experience level.

02
Attend Practices

Join scheduled practices at our Davis locations. Expert coaches guide every swimmer through structured, progressive training.

03
Get Geared Up

Grab your team suit and required training equipment. Check our gear guide for everything your swimmer needs.

04
Race & Shine

Compete in Sacramento Swim League dual meets, championship events, and USA Swimming meets throughout the year.

What Sets AquaMonsters Apart

We go beyond lap times. Our mission is developing well-rounded athletes who love the water for life.

Learn About Our Team
Expert Coaching

Individualized, technique-focused instruction at every practice to help each swimmer reach their potential.

Safe Sport

Committed to USA Swimming's Safe Sport standards — a safe, respectful environment for every athlete.

Inclusive Culture

Every swimmer belongs here — from first-time Little Monsters to competitive national-level athletes.

Progressive Training

A structured pathway from comfort in the water to proficiency in all 4 competitive strokes.

Year-Round Access

50-week schedules for USA swimmers and multiple sessions for recreation swimmers — training never stops.

College Pathways

Many of our swimmers go on to compete at the collegiate level. We're proud of every AquaMonster who swims beyond Davis.
