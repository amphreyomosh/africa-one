Our Mission

United by Faith.
Strengthened by Sisterhood.

The Young Ladies' Grand Institute (YLI) is a Catholic women's organization rooted in the Christian principles of charity and love. Founded on September 5, 1887 in San Francisco by Annie M. Sweeney, her sister Mary E. Richardson, and Emily Coogan, YLI began with a simple act of generosity—rallying to support a young woman in need.

Today, YLI continues that legacy across California, Oregon, Washington, and Hawaii, with over 5,000 members gathering in local Institutes to reflect the virtues of faith, hope, and charity at every meeting.

"The principles of unity, sisterly love and protection are the diamonds that give brilliance to our Order—they embody our love and concern for each other."
