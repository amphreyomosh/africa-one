Who We Are

A cornerstone of the
South Sacramento community.

For over 25 years, Daughters of Zion Enterpryz has stood as a vital part of the impoverished communities we serve. Our mission is to prepare youth and adults to become leaders and change agents — reducing homelessness, fighting food deprivation, and rebuilding families from the ground up.

Through weekly, monthly, and quarterly learning programs, we equip youth to succeed academically and adults to succeed professionally. It's a holistic approach that strengthens families and, ultimately, our communities.

What We Do

Programs designed to
uplift entire communities.

Three core initiatives anchor our work — meeting urgent needs today while investing in the leaders of tomorrow.

Weekly Service

Food & Clothes Closet

For more than ten years, DOZ has partnered with Sacramento Food Bank and Family Services and The Emergency Food Assistance Program to provide nutritious food, clothing, and diapers to underserved families across the Sacramento region.

Wednesdays · 4:30 – 6:30 PM · Drive-through

Youth Development

Space of Grace Youth Academy

A subdivision of DOZ Enterpryz embracing the talent, wit, and enthusiasm of our next generation. Through programs and events, we channel and refine our youth's capabilities — empowering them to lead successful lives.

Ongoing programs for ages 6 – 18
