Our Mission

More than a game. A community that grows together.

The Twelve Bridges Jr. Rhinos exist to develop young athletes through football, cheer, and STUNT — instilling discipline, respect, and confidence that lasts well beyond the final whistle.

As a feeder program for Twelve Bridges High School, we prepare every athlete to compete with pride and integrity at the next level, while building lifelong friendships along the way.

Tackle Football

Full-pads tackle football for ages 7–14 across four age divisions: Jr. Pee Wee, Pee Wee, Jr. Midgets, and Midgets. Helmet, pads, and game uniform included.

Sideline Cheer

From Mascots to Midgets, our cheer program builds skills, performance, and team spirit. Includes Jamz Camp, TBHS Clinic, league competition, and spirit pack.

Cost$200–$500

Competitive STUNT

Fast-growing competitive sport that takes cheerleading skills to the next level. Athletes compete head-to-head in partner stunts, pyramids, jumps, tumbling, and team performance.
