Who We Are

An Alliance Built for the Climate Challenge

ZNE Alliance is a nonprofit organization partnering with leading communities and organizations to advance a zero net emissions future. We deploy integrative approaches to rapid decarbonization at city and regional scales — working across the domains of clean energy, the built environment, sustainable mobility, and climate finance.

Our current projects span Advanced Energy Community initiatives in Richmond and Lancaster, California, fleet electrification and EV planning programs throughout the state, and regional climate finance strategies in the greater San Francisco Bay Area and beyond.
