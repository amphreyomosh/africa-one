About IASD

A community of dreamers, researchers, and healers.

Do you have a question about dreams or nightmares? IASD connects you to a global network of experts. Explore articles on lucid dreaming, dream telepathy, dream films, and more through our Dream FAQ. Connect with peers on our Facebook Group and attend a Regional or International Conference.

IASD publishes the peer-reviewed Dreaming journal, the DreamTime magazine, and the Dream News newsletter — providing the deepest, most current scholarship on dreams available anywhere.

Our Mission

IASD is a non-profit, international, multidisciplinary organization dedicated to the pure and applied investigation of dreams and dreaming. We promote awareness and appreciation of dreams in professional and public arenas; encourage research into the nature, function, and significance of dreaming; and provide a forum for eclectic and interdisciplinary exchange.
