Who We Are

A Club Built on Passion, Community, and Compete

Since 1977, Davis Water Polo Club has been the home for athletes who want to develop their skills, compete at a high level, and be part of a supportive team culture.

Open to girls and boys ages 5 to 18, as well as college and adult players — we offer a pathway for every level of athlete.

Competitive age-group play at regional & national levels
Experienced coaches who care about player growth
Welcoming community for beginners and elites alike
Year-round training with seasonal tournaments
Our Programs

Find Your Place in the Water

From first splash to elite competition, we have a program designed to challenge and grow every athlete.

Youth
Youth Development
Introductory water polo for ages 5–12. Build water confidence, fundamental skills, and a love of the game in a fun, supportive environment.
Competitive
Competitive Age Group
Structured training and tournament play for dedicated athletes in the 12U, 14U, 16U, and 18U divisions with regional and national competition.
Adult
Adult & College
Stay active and competitive beyond high school. Our adult program welcomes all skill levels for league play and pick-up games.

Why Choose Us

More Than a Club — A Community

Davis Water Polo Club goes beyond the pool to build character, fitness, and lifelong friendships.

Proven Track Record
Nearly five decades of developing athletes who go on to compete at the high school, collegiate, and even national levels. Our alumni speak for the program's quality.
Expert Coaching Staff
Our coaches bring deep knowledge of the game alongside a genuine commitment to each player's development — technically, athletically, and as a person.
Year-Round Programming
From summer sessions to fall and spring leagues, we keep athletes engaged and improving throughout the year with structured practice schedules and competitive opportunities.
