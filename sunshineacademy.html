Sunshine Academy · Falls Church, VA

Children are our sunshine. Every single one.

Our mission is to help all children, regardless of their socioeconomic background, across Fairfax County and neighboring communities — to pursue their academic interests and build lasting skills in reading, writing, mathematics, critical thinking, and foreign language.

Sunshine Academy serves students from Grade 1 through Grade 12. All our teachers are certified professional educators with years of classroom experience — specifically requested and praised by both parents and students for their dedication and effectiveness. Instruction is tailored to each individual student, with multimedia supplementing hands-on teaching and learning.
