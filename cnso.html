Our Vision

Setting the standard for school nursing in California.

CSNO champions the essential role of the school nurse in improving student outcomes across every district in the state.

Our Mission

CSNO promotes excellence in school nursing by optimizing student health and collaborative learning through regional grassroots efforts, professional development, leadership cultivation, evidence-based research, standards of care, and advocacy.

Who We Are

A statewide community built on the LEAD framework

As a 501(c)(3) nonprofit and an approved provider of continuing education through the California Board of Registered Nursing, CSNO has supported the profession for over seven decades. Four principles guide everything we do.

L

Leadership

Cultivating school nurse leaders who shape the future of student health.

E

Education

Delivering professional development grounded in evidence-based practice.

A

Advocacy

Representing school nurses in policy and legislation that affect students.

D

Diversity

Building an inclusive profession that reflects the communities we serve.

What We Offer

Programs that strengthen the profession

From credentialing pathways to statewide advocacy, CSNO provides the training, resources, and representation school nurses need to thrive.

School Nurse Academy

A hybrid program designed by school nurses, for school nurses — offering 62 BRN contact hours across role, legal, and clinical modules.

Explore the Academy

High Road Training Project

Competitive grant-funded credentialing slots that bring new school nurses into high-need, rural, and underserved California communities.

Professional Learning

Webinars, credentialing pathways, audiometry training, and continuing education to keep school nurses current and confident.
