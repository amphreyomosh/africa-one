1994 Founded
We Are IMBA

Rooted in Heritage,
Driven by Community

Founded in 1994 by Yolanda Ramos-Colosio in Sacramento, IMBA — Instituto Mazatlán de Bellas Artes — has been a distinguished school of dance and performing arts for over 30 years. IMBA quickly emerged as a vibrant platform, inviting Sacramento's youth to explore their Mexican heritage and unleash their kinetic and artistic talents.

Community First

Providing the most affordable performing arts education in the Sacramento area to ensure accessibility for all.

Cultural Preservation

Honoring the richness of Mexican heritage by passing traditions of dance and music to each new generation.

Inclusive Artistic Growth

A welcoming environment for all ages and backgrounds — from first-time learners to experienced performers.
