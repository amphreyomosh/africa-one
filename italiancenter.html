Preserving Italian Culture
for Every Generation

A nonprofit 501(c)(3) organization dedicated to promoting Italian language, culture, heritage, and community in the Sacramento region — for over 36 years.

Become a Member Upcoming Events

Our Mission: The Italian Cultural Society serves the cultural needs of our community, promoting and preserving the study of Italian language and culture through programs and activities found nowhere else in our region. We are a nonprofit 501(c)(3) organization.

Standing Up for Our Heritage

Our Advocacy Work

Full Advocacy

Saving Columbus Day

We are actively working to preserve the recognition of Columbus Day, celebrating the contributions of Italian Americans to the United States and their place in our national story.

Read More

Italians in Curriculum

Advocating for the inclusion of Italian and Italian-American history, contributions, and culture in California's public school curriculum — ensuring future generations know this heritage.

Read More

CA Italian American Task Force

The Society actively participates in the California Italian American Task Force, working at the state level to promote and protect the interests and heritage of Italian Americans across California.
