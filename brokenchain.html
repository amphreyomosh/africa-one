Welcome to Our Mission

Breaking Chains, Building Hope

Serving the impoverished Mayan communities of Guatemala through medical clinics, education, spiritual guidance, and compassionate humanitarian service.

Support Our Mission

Our Story

A Mission Built on Faith

Since 2006, volunteers have traveled to the remote mountains of Jalapa, Guatemala to serve communities still recovering from decades of conflict. In 2010, Broken Chains for Humanity was established as a nonprofit organization dedicated to bringing hope and healing to the Mayan people.

Medical Clinics — Free healthcare and dental services to over 800 patients annually

Food & Relief — Providing nutrition to families living in extreme poverty

Spiritual Growth — Grace-centered Bible seminars and evangelical outreach

Education — Long-term goal to build schools for Mayan children

What We Do

Our Focus Areas

We address the critical needs of impoverished Guatemalan communities through comprehensive programs.

Medical Services

Doctors, nurses, and medical professionals provide free clinics treating hundreds of patients. We're expanding mobile clinics to reach remote villages.

Food & Survival

Families in the Jalapa garbage dump search for food daily. We distribute nutrition and relief to those in extreme need.

Gospel Message

Evening Bible seminars point people toward a compassionate God, sharing the message of Christ's love and hope for eternal life.

Our Vision

The Dream

We envision a transformed future for Guatemala's most vulnerable communities. Our long-term goals include sustained medical care, quality education, and spiritual growth.

Mobile Clinics

We're accepting pre-owned ambulances and vans to convert into mobile medical and dental clinics. Trained local personnel will visit towns monthly.

Educational Centers

A long-term goal is to build schools specially designed for Mayan children, providing quality education rooted in Christian values.

Sustainable Impact

Training local medical and educational personnel ensures long-term sustainability and deeper community engagement.

Your Partnership Makes a Difference

Join us in our mission to serve the impoverished Mayan communities of Guatemala. Whether through prayer, volunteering, or financial support, your involvement transforms lives.
