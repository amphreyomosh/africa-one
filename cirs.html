Who we are

A research home for rural California's hardest questions.

Born in 1977 in the wake of agriculture's mechanization upheaval, CIRS has spent nearly five decades feeding rigorous, ground-truthed data to the movements working toward rural justice. We bridge social science research with the wisdom of farmworkers, small farmers, and the communities most often unheard.

Today, we operate from a distributed network of rural offices, working in deep partnership with the communities we study — never about them, but with them.
