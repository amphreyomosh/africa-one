About the Committee

Oversight built to protect breeders, owners, and the sport

Established by law, the California Standardbred Sires Stakes Committee (CSSSC, Inc.) administers the Sires Stakes Fund and every program connected to it. Our work ensures that investment in California-bred standardbreds is recognized, rewarded, and safeguarded across generations.

The CSSSC Board meets regularly — generally in conjunction with the California Harness Horsemen's Association board meetings — to govern eligibility rules, set race conditions, and direct purses and awards with transparency and consistency.

Statutory Authority

Created by law to oversee and administer the Sires Stakes Fund for California.

Transparent Governance

A standing board sets eligibility, race conditions, purses, and awards.

Industry Partnership

Works alongside the California Harness Horsemen's Association statewide.

Programs & Services

Four pillars of the Sires Stakes program

Every CSSSC program works together to support California's standardbred breeding industry — from registering a stallion to paying out series purses.

01

California Stallion Register

The official register of stallions standing in California, establishing eligibility for the Sires Stakes program.

02

Eligibility & Staked Generations

Clear rules defining which horses qualify and how staked generations carry forward year to year.

03

Sires Stakes Series Races

A full schedule of series races for eligible trotters and pacers, run on Sires Stakes race nights.

04

Purses & Awards

Administration of purses and awards that reward competitive performance and breeding investment.

Register your stallion and protect your investment
