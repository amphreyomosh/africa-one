Our Mission: Through a united voice and cooperative actions, PEPA enhances the viability of the poultry and egg industries in the eleven Western States and Canada — and their ability to produce and market the highest quality food products to consumers in a competitive manner.

What We Do

Six Ways PEPA Supports Your Business

From legislative advocacy to insurance programs, PEPA delivers tangible value to egg and poultry producers across the West.

Legislative Representation

PEPA monitors and represents industry interests on thousands of bills each session — from agricultural marketing and trucking to air pollution laws and farm animal welfare.

Industry Education

Seminars, conventions, and educational materials keep members current on industry trends, regulations, and best practices. PEPA's Scholarship Foundation funds future industry leaders.

Environmental & Safety Consultation

Expert staff assistance with environmental and safety compliance, including Storm Water Run-Off Regulation, pertinent to egg and poultry operations. Answers are a phone call away.

Food Safety Leadership

California's Egg Quality Assurance Plan (CEQAP), administered by PEPA, has served as the national model — helping reduce the risk of foodborne illnesses across the supply chain.

Publications & Directory

Up-to-date industry news via newsletters and special mailings. An annual directory of firms in 11 western states and British Columbia keeps members connected and informed.

Group Insurance Programs

Money-saving group insurance options including Worker's Compensation, Health & Major Medical, Dental, Disability, Life Insurance, and Property & Casualty/General Liability.

CEQAP – National Model
Food Safety Program

California Egg Quality Assurance Plan

CEQAP is a voluntary quality assurance program developed to reduce the risk of Salmonella Enteritidis in shell eggs. California's program has been recognized as the national model for egg quality assurance.

  • Reduces risk of Salmonella Enteritidis in shell eggs
  • Recognized as the national model for egg quality assurance plans
  • Voluntary program open to all California shell egg producers
  • Helps protect consumers and strengthen industry credibility
