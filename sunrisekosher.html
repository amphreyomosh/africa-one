Our Mission

Upholding the Highest Standards of Kosher Integrity

Sunrise Kosher, the Vaad of Northern California, is the region's most trusted kosher certification agency. We work directly with restaurants, caterers, markets, and food production facilities to verify and maintain standards that meet or exceed those set by leading kosher organizations worldwide.

Our certification is a guarantee — not just a label. When consumers see the Sunrise Kosher mark, they can trust that every ingredient, every process, and every facility has been rigorously reviewed and approved by our rabbinical team.

"Sunrise Kosher's standards equal, or exceed, those set for the kosher industry by the leading kosher certification agencies. Consumers can be assured of the highest kosher standards acceptable by all major kosher organizations around the world."

— Rabbi BenTzion Welton
