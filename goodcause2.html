Our Mission

"To enhance the dignity and quality of life for individuals, families and communities."

Good Cause Sacramento is a conduit for positive change, operating under Peacemakers Love Foundation, Inc. We believe in People Helping People Help Themselves — a philosophy of restoration, purpose, compassion, and faith that leads to a brighter future for every individual in our care.

Why We Do This

Restoring unity to the community

Since 2011, Good Cause Sacramento has established a self-help process that promotes stability, sobriety, and sustainability. The Life Transitions Process encourages individuals to become the best versions of themselves — with a minimum stay of 3 months and an open door for up to 2 years, or until ready.

Safe, Stable Housing A real home environment where recovery can take root and grow.
Community Support A network of peers, mentors, and volunteers who walk the journey together.
Long-term Empowerment Skills, accountability, and purpose to sustain independence after leaving our homes.
