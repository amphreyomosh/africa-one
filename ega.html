Who We Are

Advocating for Educators, Students, and Schools

The Elk Grove Education Association (EGEA) is a local chapter of the California Teachers Association (CTA) and the National Education Association (NEA). We represent teachers, nurses, counselors, librarians, and speech and language therapists across the Elk Grove Unified School District — working every day to protect your rights and elevate your profession.

Contract Protection Negotiating fair agreements for all members

Your voice is stronger with us

As a member of EGEA, CTA, and NEA, you gain access to legal protections, professional development, financial benefits, and a community of fellow educators committed to making public education the best it can be.

Legal Representation CTA attorneys to support you in disciplinary matters and evaluation disputes
Liability Insurance Comprehensive professional liability coverage through NEA and CTA
Member Benefits & Discounts Travel, insurance, financial planning, and more through CTA and NEA
Collective Bargaining Power United voice in salary negotiations, working conditions, and benefits
