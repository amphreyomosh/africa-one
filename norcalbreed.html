NorCal Bully Breed Rescue is a volunteer-run 501(c)(3) organization dedicated to rescuing pit bulls, American bulldogs, and bully-mix dogs from shelters and owner surrenders across Northern California. We rely entirely on our network of foster families to provide safe, loving temporary homes.

Meet Fig

Bully Mix  •  Young Adult  •  Sacramento Area

Fig is a sweet, affectionate bully mix who came to us from a local shelter. She's a total cuddle bug who loves people and is ready to find her forever home. Fig has been fully vetted, spayed, and is up to date on all vaccinations.

She does well in a calm household and would thrive with a patient adopter who has time to help her continue to grow. If you've been looking for a loyal, loving companion, Fig is waiting for you.

Open Your Home

Fostering saves lives. Could you be next?

Without foster homes, we cannot rescue more dogs. It is truly that simple. Every open foster home means another dog can be pulled from a shelter and given the chance to find their family.

We provide everything you need — food, supplies, veterinary care, and ongoing support from our experienced team. All you provide is time, space, and love.

  • No experience required — we train and support every foster
  • All vet costs and supplies are covered by NCBBR
  • Short or long-term fostering options available
  • Foster a dog until they find their forever home
