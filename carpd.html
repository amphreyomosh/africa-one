About CARPD

Stronger parks. Stronger communities.

The California Association of Recreation and Park Districts is a 501(c)(6) non-profit representing special recreation and park districts throughout the state. For over fifty years, we've worked to improve the quality and efficiency of the districts that serve California's communities.

Through advocacy, education, and member services — including reduced rates on workers' compensation and liability coverage via CAPRI — we help districts deliver essential, life-enhancing experiences to the people they serve.
