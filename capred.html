Who We Are

A non-profit voice for health, movement, and learning — built by educators, for educators.

Founded in 1930, the California Association for Health, Physical Education, Recreation and Dance is a 501(c)(3) voluntary membership corporation. We promote the organizing and progress of school, community, and statewide programs that put healthful living at the center of California's classrooms.

Governed by our members through councils and geographic representation, we are powered by the people who teach, coach, lead, and inspire every day.
