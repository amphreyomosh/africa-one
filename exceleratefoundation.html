Who we are

Healthier, better-educated, more connected local communities — a means to a better nation and world.

The Excelerate Foundation supports nonprofit organizations that motivate positive social change and community development at the local level.

We engage with organizations that address pressing problems and opportunities across four areas of focus, partnering with leaders who deliver lasting impact in the places they serve.

How we fund

A considered, partner-first process.

Our giving is intentional. We seek organizations whose work is rooted in community, accountable to results, and built to last.

01

Identify

We look for organizations addressing pressing local problems with credible, replicable approaches.

02

Engage

We get to know leadership and programs directly — listening before we decide.

03

Partner

We provide flexible support and stay engaged across the life of the work.

04

Sustain

We track outcomes alongside our partners and look for ways to extend impact.
