A simple goal:
Less waste, more creativity.

ReCreate is a Rocklin-based nonprofit with a straightforward mission — collect materials no longer needed by local businesses, and put them back to work in the hands of artists, educators, and kids.

We run a reuse warehouse, a Creative Maker Studio, and education programs that reach schools across the region. Everything we do connects the dots between sustainability and hands-on learning.

Teaching the 3 R's through hands-on science and art

ReCreate's education model bridges environmental responsibility with the curiosity of making. Students don't just hear about sustainability — they live it, building real projects from real diverted materials.

  • Preschool & kindergarten classroom visits
  • Field trips to our Art and Maker Studio
  • In-school and after-school STEAM enrichment
  • Sort Smart, Recycle Right assemblies
  • Family STEAM Night events
Education Programs

Our Reuse Warehouse is full of treasures.

ReCreate collects materials from local businesses that no longer need them — foam, fabric, wood, electronics parts, paper goods, and things you can't even imagine — and sells them at incredibly low prices.

Artists, teachers, sewers, costume-makers, and curious tinkerers shop here for supplies that spark projects you'd never plan in a regular art supply store.
