Our Mission

More Than Basketball.
A Community.

Youth Basketball Academy — YBA Dawgs — is dedicated to developing young athletes on and off the court. As an official Nike Team Partner, we combine elite training with character-building to produce players who compete with integrity.

From AAU competitive circuits to structured in-house leagues and summer clinics, every YBA program is built around the idea that discipline, teamwork, and hard work are the foundation of lifelong success.

What We Offer

Programs Built for Every Player

Select a program that matches your level of commitment and goals. YBA has a path for every serious athlete.

Competitive

AAU Team Memberships

Join one of our competitive AAU rosters and travel to regional and national tournaments representing YBA Dawgs at the highest levels.

In-House

YBA League

Our structured in-house league provides players consistent game reps in a competitive but supportive environment throughout the season.

Development

Camps & Clinics

Skill-specific camps and clinics for shooting, ball-handling, footwork, and IQ development, led by experienced YBA coaches.

Elite

The Academy

A year-round elite pathway for players with serious aspirations — individualized training, recruiting support, and exposure to college scouts.
