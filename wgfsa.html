Our Mission

More Than a League — A Community Built on the Diamond

Since 1985, Woodland Girls Fastpitch Softball Association has been the home of competitive youth fastpitch softball in Yolo County. We partner with NORCAL Girls Softball to provide inter-league play that challenges young athletes while building the confidence, discipline, and teamwork they carry far beyond the field.

  • Expert coaching from certified, background-checked volunteers
  • Divisions for every skill level, from 6U through 16U
  • Affiliated with NORCAL Girls Softball for regional competition
  • A welcoming, family-first environment for every player
