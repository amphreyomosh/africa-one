Who We Are

Your Destination for Horseback Riding Therapy

Project R.I.D.E., Inc. provides recreational horseback riding instruction to a wide variety of individuals. Our sessions deliver meaningful therapeutic effects for those with disabilities and special needs — improving balance, coordination, focus, and confidence through the unique bond between horse and rider.

There is a magic that occurs between horse and rider that no other therapy can replicate. With 44 years of experience and PATH Intl. premier accreditation, we are proud to serve riders of all ages and abilities from the Sacramento region.

What We Offer

Programs Built Around Every Rider

Therapeutic Riding

Structured riding sessions designed to improve physical, cognitive, and emotional well-being for individuals with a wide range of disabilities and special needs.

Learn more

Equine-Assisted Activities

Ground-based activities including grooming, leading, and horsemanship education that build confidence and responsibility alongside our experienced equines.

Learn more

Skilled Student Riders

Open to riders age 3 and up with a diagnosed challenge or special need. Our instructors and certified volunteers ensure a safe, supportive environment every session.

Getting Started

Three Steps to Your First Ride

1

Submit the Form

Complete our online inquiry form with your rider's information, diagnosis, and availability. Our team will follow up within a few days.

2

Attend Assessment

A brief on-site assessment helps our certified instructors match the rider to the right horse, program level, and support team.

3

Ride

Sessions run Monday through Friday at our Elk Grove facility. Trained volunteers and certified instructors are with every rider, every time.
