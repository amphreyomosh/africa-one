Our Mission

Affordable Homes for Seniors Who've Earned Them

President Abraham Lincoln Manor II is a nonprofit HUD-subsidized housing community in Placerville, California, dedicated to enhancing the quality of life for older adults with limited income. We believe every senior deserves a safe, welcoming place to call home — without financial hardship.

Located just minutes from Highway 50 and nestled among the stunning Sierra Nevada foothills of El Dorado County, our residents enjoy both tranquil surroundings and easy access to the amenities of historic Placerville.

As part of the Eskaton family — Northern California's nonprofit leader in senior services for over 55 years — we bring deep expertise and genuine care to every resident's experience.

Housing Eligibility

Who Can Apply for Housing?

President Abraham Lincoln Manor II is designed for older adults who want to maintain their independence while managing housing costs. HUD subsidies mean residents typically pay only 30% of their gross income toward rent.

01
Age Requirement

Applicants must meet the community's age requirement for senior housing. Please contact us directly to confirm current qualification criteria.

02
Income Limits

This is income-restricted housing. Eligible residents are those with limited income who qualify under HUD guidelines for El Dorado County.

03
Rent Calculation

Residents pay approximately 30% of their gross income toward rent, including a utility allowance. HUD deductions for medical and care expenses may apply.
