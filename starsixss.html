Our Mission

Standing Behind Those Who Stand Between Us and Danger

The Star 6 Foundation exists to serve those who serve. When a law enforcement officer falls in the line of duty or faces a critical moment of need, their family should never stand alone. We provide direct support, advocacy, and resources to officers and their families across the region — because their sacrifice demands our commitment.

Trustworthy leadership, bravery, and honor are not just words — they are the standard we hold ourselves to in every program, every initiative, and every family we support.

What We Do

Core Programs & Initiatives

01
Line of Duty Family Assistance

Immediate financial and logistical support for families of officers who have made the ultimate sacrifice in service to their communities. We ensure no family is left to navigate grief alone.

02
Officer Wellness & Recovery

Resources and referrals for officers dealing with critical incident stress, physical injury recovery, and mental health challenges. Confidential and judgement-free.

03
Scholarship Program

Educational scholarships awarded annually to children and spouses of law enforcement officers, honoring their loved one's service and sacrifice.

04
Holiday & Crisis Assistance

Targeted support during difficult periods — from holiday gift programs for children of fallen officers to emergency assistance funds when hardships arise.

The Star 6 Foundation has supported law enforcement families throughout the region since its founding.

01
End of Watch Memorial

A solemn, ongoing record honoring officers who have given their lives in the line of duty. Each name represents a commitment, a family, and a community forever changed.

02
Annual Awards Ceremony

Recognizing officers for acts of bravery, distinguished service, and community leadership. Nominations accepted from departments and the public.

03
Supporters Wall

Publicly acknowledging the individuals, businesses, and organizations who make our mission possible — a permanent record of community solidarity behind the badge.

Honoring those who served with the full recognition their sacrifice deserves.

01
Legislative Advocacy

Working with lawmakers and community leaders to strengthen protections for law enforcement officers and their families — from benefit reform to on-the-job safety standards.

02
Community Relations

Building bridges between law enforcement and the communities they serve through dialogue programs, youth outreach, and public education initiatives.

03
Professional Development

Supporting leadership training, ethics programs, and continuing education that reinforce the standards of professionalism, pride, and integrity at the core of Star 6's mission.

Advocating for those on the front lines requires sustained, organized effort at every level.

End of Watch

Their Watch Has Ended.
Our Duty Has Not.

Every officer who falls in the line of duty leaves behind a legacy that extends far beyond their final shift. The Star 6 Foundation maintains a permanent memorial and active support network for the families left behind — because honoring a life of service means continuing to serve those they loved.
