Our Issues

Catholic Teaching on the Issues Facing Our State

We bring the moral framework of Catholic social teaching to bear on Connecticut's most pressing public policy questions.

Immigration & Migration

Advocating for humane, just immigration policies rooted in the dignity of every human person.

Life & Human Dignity

Upholding the sanctity of human life from conception to natural death in state and federal law.

Religious Freedom

Defending the first freedom — the right of individuals and institutions to live by their faith.
