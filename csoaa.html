Our Goal

A responsible, unified outdoor advertising industry

The guiding principle of CSOAA and its member companies is to lead and unite a responsible outdoor advertising industry — one that serves the needs of advertisers, consumers, the public, and the governmental entities responsible for overseeing industry activities.

We are committed to doing it all in an ethical and professional manner, setting the standard for outdoor advertising across California.

Ethical LeadershipHolding our industry to the highest professional standards.
A United VoiceRepresenting member companies and affiliates statewide.
Public ServiceServing communities, advertisers, and the public good.

Public Service

The CSOAA Public Service Announcement Program

Our PSA Program coordinates CSOAA members to give charities and civic organizations the opportunity to run Public Service Announcement campaigns on outdoor advertising displays across California.

  • Donated billboard space for qualifying nonprofits
  • Coordinated statewide campaign placement
  • Greater visibility for civic and charitable causes
