Who We Are

A member-governed harbor on one of North America's clearest lakes.

The Tahoe Keys Beach and Harbor Association operates one of the largest not-for-profit docks on Lake Tahoe — 266 secured slips, a 120-vehicle lighted parking facility, and private dockside restrooms, all managed exclusively for qualifying property owners.

Our facilities run seasonally from April 1 through October 31, weather permitting. Membership requires property ownership within Tahoe Island Park Addition Number 4, including Sky Meadows and Mount Tallac Village areas.

266 Secured Boat Slips

Our slips are located at 2415 Venice Drive East, South Lake Tahoe, on the East Cove waterways shared with TKPOA and the Tahoe Keys Marina. Season runs April 1 – October 31, weather permitting.

  • 232 slips, 20 ft length — accommodating boats up to 25 ft overall
  • 8 slips, 28 ft length — accommodating boats up to 30 ft overall
  • 26 slips, 30 ft length — accommodating boats up to 32 ft overall
  • Each slip includes limited electrical power for occasional maintenance
  • Lighted parking for approximately 120 vehicles
  • Secure private restrooms, dockside access

Free Boat Launch Privilege

As a TKBHA member, there is no charge to launch your personal boat at the Tahoe Keys Marina. This exclusive privilege applies to your personal vessel only and is a member-only benefit.

Board & Member Governance

TKBHA is a member-governed nonprofit association. Our board meets monthly via virtual meeting and maintains full financial transparency with members in good standing.

  • Governing Documents & Rules available to all members
  • Board meeting agendas and minutes published post-meeting
  • Monthly financial statements accessible to members
  • Annual forms submitted each season

Shared Marina Ecosystem

The East Cove waterways are shared with TKPOA and the Tahoe Keys Marina. While TKBHA manages member slips, the Tahoe Keys Marina and surrounding businesses offer an extensive range of additional services.

  • Boat launch services (free for TKBHA members)
  • Fuel and marine repair services
  • Watercraft sales and short-term rentals
  • Fishing charters, balloon rides, parasailing
  • Dock License For Sale listings available

Lake Tahoe · TRPA Compliance

Don't Forget Your Boat Inspection

Before launching on Lake Tahoe, all vessels must pass an aquatic invasive species inspection. TKBHA members are responsible for compliance with TRPA boating regulations — inspections are mandatory and protect the lake's exceptional water clarity.
