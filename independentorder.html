Who We Are

A Fraternal Order Built on Character and Community

"We exist to make good men better, and to be a visible force of goodness in this community."

The Independent Order of Odd Fellows is a non-political, non-sectarian fraternal organization that traces its American roots to Baltimore in 1819. Morning Star Lodge No. 20 was chartered in 1852 and has maintained an unbroken record of fellowship and charitable service in Springfield and the surrounding region.

We are a diverse group of men and women united by shared values: a commitment to caring for one another, a dedication to improving our community, and a belief that together, ordinary people can accomplish extraordinary things. Our lodge hall is a gathering place for people who want to live with intention.

Visit the sick — We care for members and community neighbors in times of illness and hardship.
Relieve the distressed — We mobilize resources to aid families facing difficult circumstances.
Bury the dead — We honor the memory of those who have passed with dignity and reverence.
Educate the orphan — We invest in young people who need a path forward, regardless of their origins.

Programs & Initiatives

Putting Our Principles Into Action

From weekly food distribution to annual scholarship awards, our programs serve hundreds of neighbors every year — fueled entirely by member volunteers and community support.

Flagship Initiative

Springfield Community Food Pantry

Every Thursday, our members operate a free food pantry serving more than 180 local families. What began in 1987 as a modest donation drive has grown into one of the region's most reliable emergency food resources, distributing over 22,000 pounds of food annually. All residents are welcome, no questions asked.

Learn about the pantry
Education

Youth Excellence Scholarship Program

Since 1965, Morning Star Lodge has awarded annual college scholarships to deserving students in Springfield County based on academic merit, community service, and financial need. This year we awarded six scholarships totaling $18,000.

Senior Services

Neighbors in Need Senior Assistance

Our volunteer corps provides home visits, transportation to medical appointments, grocery delivery, and friendly companionship to isolated seniors in our community. We currently serve 62 enrolled seniors across Springfield.

Our History

172 Years of Fellowship and Service

1852
Charter Granted
Morning Star Lodge No. 20 receives its charter from the Sovereign Grand Lodge, with 14 founding members meeting in the back room of a local merchant's store on Main Street.
1878
The Lodge Hall Is Built
After years of rented quarters, members fund and build the original Fraternal Hall on Dewey Avenue, which still stands today as a historic landmark.
1919
Centennial of IOOF in America
Morning Star Lodge plays host to a statewide centennial observance, welcoming delegates from over 40 lodges and raising funds to establish a local orphanage trust.
1965
Scholarship Fund Established
The Youth Excellence Scholarship Program is created to support first-generation college students in the community, a tradition that continues to this day.
2002
Lodge Hall Restoration
A major renovation of the historic Fraternal Hall preserves the building for the next century, with members contributing labor and funds for the project.
Today
A Living Tradition
With over 340 active members and a robust program calendar, Morning Star Lodge continues its mission with the same spirit that animated its founders in 1852.

We meet upon the level, part upon the square, and part to meet again as brothers and sisters. This is not merely a saying — it is how we live with each other and with our neighbors.

— Noble Grand Elaine Hoffmann, Annual Address 2023

The Odd Fellows emerged in 18th-century England as a mutual aid society for working people who were outside the established trade guilds. They were "odd" because it was considered unusual for ordinary people — laborers, tradesmen, craftsmen — to organize for mutual benefit and social uplift.

Thomas Wildey brought the order to America in 1819, and it spread rapidly through a young nation hungry for community and solidarity. Morning Star Lodge No. 20 was among the hundreds chartered during the order's great expansion in the mid-19th century.

Today, IOOF lodges exist in more than 30 countries. Morning Star Lodge is proud to carry that international tradition of service forward, rooted firmly in the soil of Springfield.

Join Us

Membership Is Open to All People of Good Character

The IOOF welcomes men and women of all backgrounds who share a belief in the value of community, mutual aid, and personal integrity. Membership in Morning Star Lodge is a commitment to something larger than yourself.

1
Attend a Visitor's Night
We host open houses on the first Wednesday of each month. Come meet our members, tour the hall, and ask questions. No commitment required.
2
Submit a Petition
Complete a brief petition form, available at the lodge or by mail. You'll be sponsored by one current member who has come to know you.
3
Be Balloted & Initiated
The lodge votes on all petitions at a stated meeting. If accepted, you'll receive the First Degree in a brief, meaningful initiation ceremony.
