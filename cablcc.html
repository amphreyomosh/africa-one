A statewide engine for Black economic power.

Since 1998, the California Black Chamber of Commerce Foundation has been a public charity dedicated to education, training, community growth, and youth entrepreneurial development — with partnerships that have allowed our Renaissance 21st Century Project to flourish across the state and beyond.

Today we are committed to being a resource for local and statewide communities. Our work starts with financial literacy and grows into the small businesses, coalitions, and policy advocacy that shape the future of California enterprise.
