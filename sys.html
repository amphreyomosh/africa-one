Our Mission

Advancing Talent,
Inspiring a Lifelong
Love of Music

As Sacramento's foremost orchestral youth organization, we aspire to become the region's most influential community for the advancement of talent and music appreciation among youth. Now entering our 70th year, the Sacramento Youth Symphony offers orchestral training to young musicians — from fledgling through pre-professional levels — directed by some of the most acclaimed conductors and teachers in the region.

Students learn from talented music educators and conductors, gain exposure to music history and theory, and perform challenging orchestral works in several exciting concerts every year. Depending on their ensemble, they may have the opportunity to tour locally or internationally.

Orchestras & Ensembles

All Programs
Advanced Level

Premier Orchestra

The flagship ensemble of the Sacramento Youth Symphony, the Premier Orchestra brings together the region's most advanced young string, wind, brass, and percussion players. Students perform full-scale symphonic repertoire under the direction of Conductor Ryan Murray, with opportunities for international touring and concerto solo performances.

Conductor Ryan Murray
Eligibility By audition — intermediate to advanced level
Repertoire Full symphonic works, concertos, and chamber pieces
Audition Requirements
Wind & Brass · Percussion

Symphonic Wind Ensemble

A rigorous, audition-based wind band ensemble offering advanced wind, brass, and percussion players the opportunity to study and perform challenging concert band repertoire. Students develop technique, ensemble listening, and musical leadership in a focused small-group setting with dedicated professional instruction.

Focus Wind, brass, and percussion players
Format Weekly rehearsals with semester concerts
Learn More
Intermediate Level

Academic Orchestra

The Academic Orchestra is the ideal next step for intermediate string, wind, and brass players ready to experience full orchestral performance for the first time. Students gain foundational ensemble skills, section leadership experience, and the thrill of performing full concerts in front of Sacramento audiences.

Level At least one year of instrument experience
Pathway Entry point to Premier Orchestra

Summer Chamber Music Workshop

Founded in 1990 and directed by Claire Tatman, the SYS Summer Chamber Music Workshop offers an intensive week of ensemble playing for young musicians ages 7–19. The program introduces students to chamber music literature and rehearsal techniques through individualized coaching with professional performers, culminating in a public recital.

Ages Week 1: 7–12 · Week 2: 11–19
Director Claire Tatman
Ends With Public recital for all participants
Summer Registration
Beginning Strings

Overture Strings

Overture Strings is SYS's entry-level string ensemble, welcoming young violinists, violists, cellists, and bassists at the beginning of their orchestral journey. This program builds foundational string technique, music reading, and ensemble habits that prepare students for a lifetime of musical participation.

Instruments Violin, viola, cello, bass
Pathway Leads directly into Academic Orchestra

Concert Season 2025–2026

Four Concerts. One Unforgettable Season.

Each year, Sacramento Youth Symphony ensembles take the stage in multiple concerts across the Sacramento region, presenting ambitious and beloved orchestral works to live audiences. From season-opening performances to the Spring Showcase, every concert is a milestone in each student's musical growth — and a gift to the community.
