Our Mission

A foundation built on showing up for one another.

Rejoice Care Foundation exists to put faith into practice — meeting practical needs, building relationships, and creating spaces where young people and families feel seen, supported, and loved.

From mentorship circles to food pantry distributions, every program is carried out by volunteers from local churches who believe that care is not a program, but a way of life.

What We Do

Core programs that carry our mission forward

Youth Enrichment & Spiritual Mentorship

We walk alongside young people through mentorship, Bible study, and enrichment activities designed to build character, confidence, and a strong foundation of faith for the road ahead.

Explore Youth Ministry

Feeding Ministry & Food Pantry

Through our homeless feeding initiative and ongoing food pantry, we provide consistent access to meals and groceries for individuals and families facing food insecurity in our community.

Explore Feeding Ministry

Operation Christmas Child

Each year, we partner with Samaritan's Purse, local churches, and families to pack shoebox gifts filled with toys, school supplies, and hygiene items for children around the world.

Beyond the gift itself, every shoebox carries the message of God's love, often opening doors for the gospel to reach communities that would otherwise never hear it.

Discipleship & Local Church Partnership

We come alongside local churches to nurture growth in faith through small groups, discipleship pathways, and international mission outreach that extends our care beyond our own community.
