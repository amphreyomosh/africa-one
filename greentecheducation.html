Who We Are

Workforce skills rooted in environmental action.

GREEN Tech student employees are learning workforce and entrepreneurial techniques that prepare them for green-collar careers while actively improving their communities.

1
Work Ethic & Professionalism
Students develop foundational workforce skills including discipline, teamwork, communication, and accountability.
2
Reducing Greenhouse Gases
Hands-on projects focused on clean energy, sustainability practices, and understanding environmental impact.
3
Reducing Pollution
Students engage in real community projects that address air quality and environmental health challenges.
4
Improving Low-Income Communities
GREEN Tech bridges the equity gap by bringing quality technical training to underserved Sacramento neighborhoods.

What We Offer

Programs built for real careers.

Classroom Training
An overview of the Green Collar Economy covering manufacturing, construction trades, public utilities, transportation, and environmental protection careers. Includes soft skills, vocabulary, resume writing, and financial literacy.
Learn more
Laboratory Projects
Students build and develop projects from conception to completion. Real manufacturing and construction concepts with emphasis on reducing human impact on the environment through applied learning.
Learn more
Project IGNITE
A specialized accelerator program connecting students to mentors, industry partners, and real job placements in the growing green economy — from solar energy to urban agriculture.
