Our reach

A sisterhood that spans the Bay Area and the globe.

As an affiliate of the International Women's Forum, our members are part of a worldwide community of 8,400+ accomplished women across 77 local forums in 35 countries. Locally, we serve the communities where we live and work — with a shared commitment to giving back and advancing the next generation.
