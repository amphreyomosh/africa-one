Where Northern California Comes to Race

From NHRA Summit ET Series weekends and JR Dragster divisional events, to the legendary Kool April Drags featuring Jets and Blown Alcohol Funny Cars — Redding Motorsports Park is the region's home for serious competition and unforgettable spectacle. Every season brings signature events that draw racers and fans from across California and beyond.

⚠️ Hot Weather Policy: Events may be canceled when forecast temps exceed 106°F at race time — for the safety of racers, fans, and staff.

The heart of the Park's calendar. NHRA Summit ET Series events run across multiple weekends covering Super Pro, Pro, Sportsman, Motorcycle, JR Dragsters, and more. Full bracket racing for all skill levels with NHRA points on the line.

Midnite Mayhem GrudgeFest & Skid Pit

The Park's rawest, most electric event format. Street Machine grudge racing on the strip, plus the Skid Pit for tire-shredding burnout competition. Gates open 8PM, racing from 9PM to 1AM — lights out, no excuses.

Signature Annual Event

Kool April Drags

The crown jewel of the season. The Kool April Drags is a multi-day spectacular featuring Jet Cars, Blown Alcohol Funny Cars, West Coast Outlaws, Extreme Pro Mods, and NorCal Drag Bikes. Plus a Car Show and Street Machine GrudgeFest opener. This is a can't-miss event.

Jet Cars Funny Cars Pro Mods Car Show NorCal Drag Bikes

Thunder Under The Lites

The NHRA Hot Rod Heritage Series comes to Redding with Nitro Burning Top Fuel Dragsters, Pro Comp, West Coast Outlaws, Jr Fuel Dragsters, Pro Gas Door Cars, Nostalgia Eliminator classes, and Hot Rod. A full weekend of fire, fury, and nostalgia-soaked speed.
