About CFSA

Foster ethical practice. Champion the industry. Inform the lawmaker.

CFSA exists to foster ethical practices and high standards of conduct across California’s finance industry. Through our lobbyists, legal counselors, and public-relations specialists, we connect lenders directly to the legislators and regulators shaping consumer credit policy.

Together, our members form a unified voice — bolstering individual companies with influence and resources they could not muster alone.
