About CFOT

A Nonprofit Dedicated to Advancing Occupational Therapy

The California Foundation for Occupational Therapy was founded in 1981 as a nonprofit, public benefit organization to help support students, practitioners, and researchers advance their knowledge and skills — thereby enhancing occupational therapy services to all Californians.

Programs & Funding

How CFOT Supports the OT Community

From student scholarships to practitioner research grants, CFOT offers multiple pathways to fund your OT journey.

Scholarships

Over 20 scholarships awarded annually — $3,000 each — to California OT/OTA students enrolled in accredited programs with a minimum 3.0 GPA and OTAC membership.

View scholarships

Traineeships

OTA traineeship awards for occupational therapy assistant students, enabling recipients to focus fully on their education and clinical training goals.

View traineeships

Research Grants

Funding from $300 seed money up to $10,000 general grants for OT practitioners and faculty conducting research in California. Awarded annually and quarterly.

View research funding

Seed Money

Up to $300 quarterly for pilot studies, program development projects, and capstone research. Distributed four times a year — no request is too small.

Learn more

Awardee Recognition

CFOT celebrates award recipients each year at the Annual Awards Luncheon & Symposium during the OTAC Conference — honoring excellence in OT.
