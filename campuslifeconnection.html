Who We Are

A trusted presence for youth and families since 1999

Campus Life Connection has served the greater Sacramento area for over 25 years, offering programs for children, teens and their families across a five-county region. Each year we reach thousands of young people — one relationship at a time.

Our Vision

To engage young people and their families in order to transform lives, provide hope, and effect positive change for individuals and neighborhoods.

Our Mission

To develop character, confidence, responsibility and faith in the lives of young people and their families through educational, social, physical and spiritual programs.

Character Confidence Responsibility Faith
