Who We Are

Fighting for consumers' rights — ahead of corporate profit

The Consumer Federation of California is a nonprofit advocacy organization. Each year, we testify before the California legislature on dozens of bills affecting millions of consumers, and appear before state agencies in support of consumer regulations.

Our work spans medical, financial and digital privacy; truth in advertising; toxic-chemical reform; and protecting seniors from predatory contracts — always placing people before profit.

  • Member-led: delegates set policy at a governing convention every two years
  • Funded in part by cy pres awards — used entirely to advance consumer rights
  • Supported by the CFC Education Foundation, a 501(c)(3) research organization

What We Work On

The consumer issues we champion

Corporations have lobbyists — you have CFC. These are the fights we take on at the State Capitol and before regulatory agencies.

Privacy & Data Rights

Protecting Californians' medical, financial and digital privacy — including new control over personal data and simpler account cancellations.

Predatory Lending

Cracking down on predatory payday lenders, medical credit cards and home-improvement scams that trap families in debt.

Housing & Insurance

Shielding homeowners and HOA members from massive rate hikes and working to lower home insurance costs across the state.

Antitrust & Monopolies

Co-sponsoring the COMPETE Act to modernize California's antitrust laws and challenging monopolies like Ticketmaster / Live Nation.

Truth in Advertising

Keeping California's "Made in USA" label strong and holding companies accountable for honest, transparent marketing.

Health & Safety

Removing toxic chemicals from furniture and insulation while maintaining fire safety and encouraging healthier building practices.
