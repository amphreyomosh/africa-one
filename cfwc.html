Who We Are

A fact-based voice for California agriculture

The California Farm Water Coalition is a non-profit, educational organization committed to sharing fact-based information about the important role water plays in the food, fiber, and nursery products grown in California.

Serve as the unified voice for agricultural water users across California
Represent irrigated agriculture in the media and to policymakers
Educate the public about the benefits of California's irrigated agriculture
