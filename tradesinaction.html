Who We Are

Standing Firm on the Foundations of Catholic Tradition

Tradition In Action is committed to defending the perennial Magisterium of Holy Mother Church and its centuries-old Catholic traditions. We stand against progressivist currents that seek to erode the doctrinal and moral heritage of the Church.

Our work reaches readers across the globe through in-depth analysis, historical scholarship, theological commentary, and cultural criticism — all grounded in fidelity to Catholic teaching as it has been handed down through the ages.
