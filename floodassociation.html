Our Mission

Advancing Flood Safety Across California's Central Valley

Founded in 1926, CCVFCA promotes the common interests of its member agencies in maintaining effective flood control systems throughout California's Central Valley — safeguarding life, property, and the natural environment for generations to come.

Policy Advocacy
Representing member interests at the state legislature, regulatory agencies, and in court.
Membership Network
Connecting flood control districts, reclamation districts, and agencies across the Central Valley.
Technical Resources
Providing members with up-to-date information, research, and operational guidance.

Join the Association
