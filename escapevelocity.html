Our Mission

Building a foundation for every youth to launch into their future.

Escape Velocity Resources Foundation exists to ensure that every young person in Sacramento — regardless of circumstance — has the tools, support, and opportunity needed to chart their own course.

As a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, we provide direct services and programs that address the social, educational, and economic barriers facing youth and their families. From workforce readiness to family navigation, we walk alongside our community every step of the way.

Our work is rooted in Sacramento, driven by community members who understand these challenges firsthand — because proximity matters.
