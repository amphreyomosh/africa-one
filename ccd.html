About CCCD

A nonprofit dedicated to California's cooperative movement

CCCD is a 501(c)3 nonprofit that promotes cooperatives as a vibrant business model to address the economic and social needs of California's communities. We support cooperatives of all types — agricultural, arts, childcare, energy, housing, worker-owned, and more.

Many of our staff and board members were previously associated with the University of California at Davis Center for Cooperatives, bringing decades of experience to communities across the state.
