Our Mission

More Than Gymnastics — A Foundation for Life

Woodland Gymnastics has been a cornerstone of the Woodland, CA community, offering top-tier gymnastics, cheer, and tumbling instruction for athletes from 18 months to 18 years. Our experienced coaching staff builds not just athletic ability, but confidence, discipline, and lifelong character.

We run recreational programs, competitive teams, toddler play sessions, birthday parties, open gyms, and parents' night out — everything a family needs in one fully equipped facility.

Scholarships available: qualifying families can receive 10–40% off monthly tuition. Ask at the front office.
