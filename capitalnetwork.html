About Us

A Non-Partisan Home for Capitol Women

Capitol Network is where women in the Capitol community gather to build professional connections and lasting friendships — in a welcoming, non-partisan environment with others in government and political careers.

Professional Networking
Develop meaningful working relationships with professionals throughout the Capitol community across all branches of government.
Charitable Giving
Fundraising events and sponsorships benefit community organizations that support women and children throughout California.
Career Advancement
New Professional and Corporate Memberships help women at all career stages grow their influence and expand their networks.

Mentorship

Grow Through Guided Mentorship

Our mentorship program connects experienced Capitol professionals with emerging leaders — building careers, confidence, and community.

1
Apply for the Program
Members apply to be matched as either a mentor or mentee based on their experience and career goals.
2
Get Matched
Capitol Network pairs mentors and mentees based on shared interests, career paths, and professional goals within the Capitol community.
3
Build Your Relationship
Engage in regular meetings, attend events together, and grow professionally through a meaningful, structured mentoring relationship.
