This year's theme: The Roaring Twenties

A dynamic exploration of one of the most transformative decades in modern history. Students dive into the Jazz Age through art, music, and literature — with The Great Gatsby as the featured long work — alongside algebra and trigonometry, electricity and magnetism, and the economics and culture that shaped the era.
