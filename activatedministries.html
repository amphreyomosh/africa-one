Who we are

Impatient people, putting compassion into action.

Activated Ministries is a California 501(c)(3) nonprofit established in 1999. Together, our team has spent decades living and volunteering in dozens of countries where standards of living, healthcare, sanitation, and education fall painfully short.

That experience shapes everything we do. We support both humanitarian and spiritually oriented work because people need help in both — relieving immediate hardship while also addressing the deeper causes of suffering. Lasting change, we believe, treats the whole person.

Read our full story

What guides our work

Three commitments shape every project we support around the world.

Humanitarian relief Practical aid for families facing crisis and hardship.
Education & scholarships Investing in young people as the key to lasting change.
Inspirational materials Producing and distributing resources that build character.
