Our Story

A Roseville Institution Rooted in Service

The Roseville Elks Lodge #2248 offers a wonderful gathering spot for both men and women — where people know your name. We're dedicated to community service, charitable giving, and making a difference for kids, veterans, and neighbors.

🤝
Brotherhood & Fellowship

A welcoming community where members build lasting bonds through shared values, events, and service.
