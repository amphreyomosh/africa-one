What We Do

A National Resource for Farmers Markets

FMC serves as an education center, a policy voice, and a professional network for market managers and farmers across the country.

An Education Center

FMC works to equip market managers and farmers with the tools necessary to run successful markets. We share knowledge through webinars, newsletters, and a comprehensive Resource Library backed by rigorous research.

Explore Resources
A Policy Voice

While market organizers are busy growing healthy food for their communities, FMC gives farmers markets a voice in public policies that impact our food system — ensuring local producers are represented at state and federal levels.

Our Advocacy
A Professional Network

FMC provides a supportive network where market managers, vendors, and customers can connect, share best practices, and collaborate. We work closely with regional leaders to build the capacity of state farmers market associations.
