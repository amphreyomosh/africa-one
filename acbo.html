Our Mission

Serving California's Community College Business Officers

The Association of Chief Business Officials offers business officers in California opportunities to develop professionally, to identify issues affecting community colleges, and to share their expertise with their colleagues.

Our membership spans 73 districts and 116 California community colleges, collectively enrolling 1.8 million students — making ACBO one of the most impactful professional associations in California public higher education.
