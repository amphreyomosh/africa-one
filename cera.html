About CERA

A Professional Home for California's Education Community

The California Educational Research Association (CERA) bridges research, data, and educational practice in California for the purpose of transforming insight into impact.

Founded in 1921, CERA is a non-profit, professional association with members from K–12, higher education, research organizations, and community and business partners. By bridging these often-siloed domains, CERA empowers educators and leaders to translate evidence into meaningful action.

When you join CERA, you become part of a statewide network committed to advancing equity and improving outcomes for students across California. There is power in our collective when we come together with purpose.
