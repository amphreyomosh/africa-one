Our Identity

Rooted in Alexandria,
alive in New Jersey.

SMAR Church stands as a member of the Coptic Orthodox Archdiocese in North America, under the patriarchal jurisdiction of the Coptic Orthodox Patriarchate of Alexandria — one of the oldest Christian communities on earth. Here, ancient liturgies are sung in Coptic, Arabic, and English, and families are formed across generations in the full richness of this heritage.

"For of such is the kingdom of heaven."

Our Ministries

Formed in faith
at every stage of life.

From the youngest Sunday School student to the women who gather in fellowship, every member of SMAR is called into an active life of worship, study, and community.

Sunday School

Matthew 19:14 — "Let the little children come to Me."

We seek to raise our children in the fear of God. Through dedicated servants and a curriculum rooted in Scripture, Sunday School forms the spiritual foundation of each new generation in our community. Every child is welcomed, every question honored.

Saturdays · 11:15 AM – 12:15 PM · 8 Old Rd, South Amboy

Hymns & Bible Study

Psalm 34:5 — "His praise shall continually be in my mouth."

Praise is the language of heaven. We carry the Coptic Church's ancient and rich tradition of hymnody, pairing it with rigorous study of the Holy Scriptures. Every believer is called to be fluent in both worship and the Word of God.

Fridays · 6:30 PM – 8:00 PM · 8 Old Rd, South Amboy

Ladies Meeting

Hebrews 10:24–25 — "Stir up love and good works, not forsaking the assembling of ourselves together."

We foster an environment where the faithful ladies of our church can gather, grow closer to God and to each other, encourage one another in virtue, and build the kind of deep community that stands across seasons of life.

Fridays · 6:30 PM – 8:00 PM · 8 Old Rd, South Amboy

College & Young Adults

1 Timothy 4:12 — "Be an example to the believers."

The College and Young Adults ministry is a space for young Coptic Christians to wrestle with faith, build lifelong friendships, and take ownership of their spiritual lives. Discussion, worship, and fellowship shape an authentic community of young believers.

Fridays · 8:00 PM – 10:00 PM · 8 Old Rd, South Amboy

A Living Tradition

The Coptic Church has never stopped singing.

In a world of noise and fragmentation, SMAR offers something rare: an unbroken liturgical tradition stretching back to the Apostle Mark, faithfully carried across continents and centuries, now alive here in New Jersey. Come and experience it for yourself.
