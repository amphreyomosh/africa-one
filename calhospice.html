Our Mission

Enhancing the value and quality of end-of-life care across California.

CHAPCA is a not-for-profit organization that improves access to quality end-of-life care and educates patients, families, communities, and healthcare professionals about hospice and palliative care.

We envision a future where every patient and family is aware of hospice and palliative care services—and has full, equitable access to them. The best time to learn about hospice is before it's needed.
